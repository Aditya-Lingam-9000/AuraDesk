1. PREREQUISITES & MAC / PC VIVO OFFICE KIT CONNECTION SETUP
================================================================================

Step 1.1: Connect Phone and Laptop to the Same Wi-Fi Network
  - Ensure your Vivo phone (V2502 / Funtouch OS) and your Mac/PC are connected 
    to the same Wi-Fi network (or turn on Mobile Hotspot on your Vivo phone 
    and connect your laptop to it).

Step 1.2: Establish Vivo Office Kit / EasyShare Multi-Screen Connection
  - On your Mac or PC:
    1. Open the "Vivo Office Kit" (or Vivo PC Suite / EasyShare) desktop application.
    2. A QR Code and connection prompt will be displayed on your laptop screen.
  - On your Vivo Phone:
    1. Open the built-in "EasyShare" app (or pull down Quick Settings -> "Multi-Screen" / "Vivo Office Kit").
    2. Tap "Multi-Screen Collaboration" or "Connect to PC / Mac".
    3. Scan the QR code displayed on your laptop screen.
    4. Confirm the connection prompt on both phone and laptop.
  - Verification: You should now see your phone listed or connected in the 
    Vivo Office Kit desktop window on your Mac/PC.

Step 1.3: Verify AuraDesk Ecosystem Status on Mobile
  - Open the AuraDesk application on your Vivo phone.
  - In the "Focus" tab, look at the "Vivo Office Kit & PC Suite" card.
  - Check the badges:
    * "Ecosystem Ready" (Green) - Confirms EasyShare, Vivo Notes, and PC Suite detected.
    * 4 Pillars grid: Screen Mirroring (Ready), Laptop Mute (Normal), Jovi Notes (Ready), Clipboard (Synced).

2. PILLAR 1: AUTOMATIC SCREEN MIRRORING & FOCUS BANNER TESTING
================================================================================
Goal: When you start the Focus Guard, AuraDesk automatically launches the 
      screen mirroring session with your Mac/PC and displays the Focus Banner:
      "[User Name] in Deep Work till [Return Time]. Tap phone twice to interrupt urgently."

--- Method A: Automatic Launch via "Start Guard" Button ---
  1. On your phone in AuraDesk, tap the primary "Start Guard" button.
  2. WHAT HAPPENS AUTOMATICALLY:
     - AuraDesk immediately initiates the screen mirroring pipeline via 
       `es://dl.mirroring` and `vivo.intent.action.EASYSHARE_MIRRORING`.
     - The EasyShare / Vivo Office Kit mirroring interface activates.
     - The high-priority focus banner intent `com.vivo.officekit.SCREEN_MIRROR_BANNER` 
       is broadcasted with your name and return time (calculated on-device by Qwen2-0.5B).
     - A persistent system banner notification is posted: 
       "🖥️ Laptop Screen Mirroring: Focus Protected".
  3. OBSERVATION ON MAC / PC:
     - The mirrored phone window appears on your Mac/PC desktop.
     - The Focus Banner text is visible across the mirrored display:
       "Arjun in Deep Work till [Time]. Tap phone twice to interrupt urgently."

--- Method B: In-App Direct "🖥️ Start Mirroring" Button ---
  1. Scroll to the "Vivo Office Kit & PC Suite" card in AuraDesk.
  2. Tap the "🖥️ Start Mirroring" button.
  3. OBSERVATION ON MOBILE:
     - Toast appears: "🖥️ Screen Mirroring session initiated for Arjun ([Time])".
     - The card status changes to "SHIELD MIRRORED" (Green) and "Banner Active".
  4. OBSERVATION ON MAC / PC:
     - The screen mirroring session launches and displays the focus banner.
  5. Tap "🖥️ Mirror Active" to dismiss the banner when desired.

--- Method C: Real-World Face-Down Sensor Arming ---
  1. With Guard Service running, place your Vivo phone face-down on your desk.
  2. Audio/haptic chime confirms arming.
  3. OBSERVATION ON MAC / PC:
     - Mirrored display updates to Armed Focus state with active banner.
  4. Pick your phone face-up.
  5. OBSERVATION ON MAC / PC:
     - The focus banner dismisses automatically and restores normal state.

3. PILLAR 2: TASK HANDOFF & LAPTOP NOTIFICATION MUTING TESTING
================================================================================
Goal: Completely silences distracting PC/Laptop notification popups and sounds 
      during deep work focus sprints via 3 synchronized tiers:
      1. Android System Do Not Disturb (DND Priority Mode via NotificationManager)
      2. Hardware Audio Stream Muting (AudioManager Ringer & Notification Mute)
      3. Vivo Office Kit & EasyShare Broadcast Intents (`com.vivo.officekit.MUTE_NOTIFICATIONS`)

--- Method A: In-App Mute Toggle Test ---
  1. On the "Vivo Office Kit & PC Suite" card, look at the action buttons.
  2. Tap the "🔇 Mute Laptop" button.
  3. OBSERVATION ON MOBILE:
     - Toast appears: "🔇 Laptop notifications muted via Office Kit".
     - Android Do Not Disturb (DND) icon appears in the status bar.
     - The "Laptop Notification Handoff" pill changes to "MUTED" (Amber).
     - Ringer and notification audio volumes are silenced.
     - The button label switches to "🔊 Unmute Laptop".
  4. OBSERVATION ON MAC / PC:
     - When a notification arrives, no sound or popup banner interrupts your Mac/PC.
  5. Tap "🔊 Unmute Laptop".
  6. OBSERVATION ON MOBILE & LAPTOP:
     - Toast appears: "🔊 Laptop notifications unmuted".
     - System DND is deactivated, and normal notification audio is restored.
     - The pill returns to "UNMUTED" / "Normal".

--- Method B: Automatic Focus Arming Synchronization ---
  1. Place your phone face-down -> AuraDesk engages DND and broadcasts `mute = true`.
  2. Pick your phone face-up -> AuraDesk disengages DND and restores all laptop alerts.

4. PILLAR 3: DIRECT JOVI NOTES SYNC TESTING
================================================================================
Goal: Automatically extracts tasks from voice/text interruptions and syncs rich 
      Markdown task capsules into Vivo Notes (`content://com.vivo.notes.provider/notes`) 
      and local Markdown files via FileProvider.

--- Method A: In-App "📝 Sync Jovi Note" Button ---
  1. On the "Vivo Office Kit & PC Suite" card, tap "📝 Sync Jovi Note".
  2. OBSERVATION ON MOBILE:
     - Toast appears: "Synced to Jovi Notes + Clipboard!".
     - The "Jovi Notes SQLite Sync" counter increments (e.g., "1 SYNCED").
     - The Vivo Notes app receives the formatted task capsule:
       ====================================================================
       # 📝 AuraDesk Task: Merge payment schema fix & update staging keys
       - **Requester:** Rahul (Backend Lead)
       - **Deadline:** Today 5:00 PM (Client Demo)
       - **Priority:** 🚨 URGENT / CRITICAL
       - **Captured At:** [Timestamp] (8s desk visit)
       - **Status:** Ready for Action

       ### 🎙️ Visitor Speech Transcript
       > "Hey make sure you merge the payment schema fix before client demo at 5"

       ### 💻 Focus Context Interrupted
       ```
       Editing CheckoutActivity.kt line 88
       ```
       ---
       *Auto-captured by AuraDesk Focus Guard • Synced via Vivo Office Kit*
       ====================================================================

--- Method B: Sync from Interruption Log Tab ---
  1. Switch to the "Interruption Log" tab in AuraDesk.
  2. Tap "Record 10s Capsule" or "Simulate Rahul Speech".
  3. The on-device LLM summarizes the action item and urgency.
  4. Tap the sync icon on the capsule card.
  5. Open the native Vivo "Notes" app on your phone -> verify the note is saved under "AuraDesk Focus".

--- Method C: Verify Local Markdown File Export ---
  - File is saved at: `Android/data/com.auradesk.guard/files/notes/AuraDesk_[ID]_[Task].md`

5. PILLAR 4: EASYSHARE FILE TRANSFER & CROSS-DEVICE CLIPBOARD SYNC TESTING
================================================================================
Goal: Pushes auto-replies and task summaries to the system clipboard and EasyShare 
      so you can paste them instantly on your Mac/PC laptop (Slack / Docs / IDE).

--- Method A: EasyShare Markdown Summary File Transfer ---
  1. On the "Vivo Office Kit & PC Suite" card, tap "🚀 Send EasyShare File".
  2. OBSERVATION ON MOBILE:
     - Toast appears: "🚀 EasyShare file transfer broadcasted to Mac/PC".
     - AuraDesk generates `AuraDesk_Focus_Summary.md` and shares it via EasyShare 
       and FileProvider.
  3. OBSERVATION ON MAC / PC:
     - The file arrives in your Vivo Office Kit / EasyShare transfer inbox.

--- Method B: Cross-Device Clipboard Sync (Phone to Mac/PC) ---
  1. On your phone, tap "📝 Sync Jovi Note" (or trigger an auto-reply).
  2. The text is copied to Android clipboard with `EXTRA_IS_SENSITIVE = false`.
  3. ON YOUR MAC OR PC:
     - Click inside any text box (e.g., Slack, Notes, TextEdit, Notepad, or Browser).
     - Press `Cmd + V` (on Mac) or `Ctrl + V` (on Windows).
     - OBSERVATION: The full formatted task markdown or auto-reply text pastes 
       instantly on your laptop screen!

7. CLI / ADB TERMINAL TESTING COMMANDS (FOR TECHNICAL VERIFICATION)
================================================================================

Run these commands from your computer terminal (with phone connected via USB or Wi-Fi adb):

A. Trigger Vivo Screen Mirroring via DeepLink:
   adb shell am start -a android.intent.action.VIEW -d "es://dl.mirroring"

B. Broadcast Official Screen Mirror Focus Banner Intent:
   adb shell am broadcast -a com.vivo.officekit.SCREEN_MIRROR_BANNER \
     --es text "Arjun in Deep Work till 12:30 PM. Tap phone twice to interrupt urgently." \
     --es user_name "Arjun" --es return_time "12:30 PM" --ez is_armed true

C. Broadcast Official Laptop Notification Mute Intent:
   adb shell am broadcast -a com.vivo.officekit.MUTE_NOTIFICATIONS --ez mute true

D. Trigger On-Device LLM Auto-Reply & Office Kit Sync:
   adb shell am broadcast -p com.auradesk.guard -a com.auradesk.guard.TEST_LLM \
     --es message "Did you review the payment schema PR?" --es sender "Vikram"

E. Monitor Live Vivo Office Kit & LLM Logs in Terminal:
   adb logcat -s VivoOfficeKitManager LlamaModelRunner FocusNotifListener
